Privacy Policy

Root Node operates under a strict principle of data minimization. We do not monetize reader attention through third-party ad brokers or cross-site tracking beacons.

1. Introduction and Scope

Root Node (“we”, “our”, or “the Publication”) is an independent technical journal accessible via rootnode.dev and its mirrors. This document articulates what technical metadata is captured when you browse our pages, how submitted correspondence is processed, and our commitment to reader autonomy.

2. Technical Log Data and Server Infrastructure

Like virtually all internet web hosts, our origin servers and content delivery networks (CDNs) temporarily record standard HTTP access logs to maintain uptime, protect against distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) intrusions, and resolve routing anomalies. These logs typically capture:

  • Your public Internet Protocol (IP) address.
  • Browser user-agent string and operating system architecture.
  • Requested uniform resource identifier (URI) path and HTTP status code.
  • HTTP Referer header (indicating the previous link followed, if transmitted by your browser).
  • Timestamp of request fulfillment.

These logs are retained strictly for operational security and debugging diagnostics, typically purged on an automated rolling schedule of 14 to 30 days. They are never collated with external commercial identity databases.

3. Communications & Contact Desk

When you communicate with Root Node through our editorial contact form or direct email correspondence, you voluntarily provide your name, email address, and message content. This information is utilized solely to evaluate your query, coordinate errata corrections, or answer technical inquiries. We will never sell, rent, or share your email address with marketing syndicates.

4. Newsletter Subscriptions

If you opt into our publication newsletter, your email address is recorded solely to transmit our dispatches. Every transmission includes an unambiguous, automated unsubscribe link. We do not embed hidden tracking pixels designed to monitor individual email open rates or IP-based read locations.

5. Cookies and Local Storage

Root Node does not set non-essential marketing cookies, behavioral retargeting trackers, or cross-domain advertising scripts. We may use standard browser local storage (`localStorage`) strictly to preserve client-side display preferences (such as reading typography settings or high-contrast adjustments) on your personal device.

6. Third-Party Links & Exterior Services

Our long-form educational essays frequently link to official documentation (e.g., IETF RFC standards, kernel archives, MDN Web Docs, and GitHub source repositories). Once you click an outbound link to an exterior domain, that third party’s privacy policy governs your interaction.

7. Reader Rights (GDPR & CCPA Alignment)

Regardless of your geographic jurisdiction, you have the right to request confirmation of whether we hold any personal information regarding your email correspondence, request its correction, or demand its permanent deletion. Inquiries may be addressed to our privacy desk at privacy@rootnode.internal.

8. Updates to this Policy

Should our infrastructure stack or data collection protocols change, this document will be updated with an updated revision date. Material changes will be highlighted on our homepage.
